      How this works

      1. One scan each morning covers all ten cities: local-language media, international outlets and university sources.
      2. Each development is tagged once by city, topic, signal level and the teams it matters to.
      3. Each team reads its own view. Communications sees reputation and media, Operations sees travel and security, Academic Programs sees partners and peers.
      4. Items outside a view stay one click away, so nobody needs a separate alert.

      Situational awareness only. It does not replace official emergency alerts or confirmation from Center colleagues.

      Each development lists its outlet and publication date. Keyboard: 1 to 4 switch team view, / search, Esc close a panel, ← → move between cities in a city panel.
