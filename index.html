      How this works

      1. One scan each morning covers all ten cities: local media in the local language, international outlets and university sources.
      2. Each development is tagged once: city, category, signal level and which teams it matters to.
      3. Each team reads its own view. Communications sees reputation and media. Operations sees travel, security and closures. Academic Programs sees partners, peers and higher education.
      4. Items outside a view stay one click away, so nothing is lost and nobody has to set up a separate alert.

      This is situational awareness. It does not replace official emergency alerts or direct confirmation from Center colleagues.
